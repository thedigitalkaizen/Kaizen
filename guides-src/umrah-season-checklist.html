Umrah Season Campaign Checklist

Everything an Umrah travel agency should have ready before peak season: packages, ads, WhatsApp follow-up and trust signals. Tick it off, step by step.

thekaizenagency.com · Ramadan 2027 edition
Key dates: Ramadan 1448 AH is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, with Eid al-Fitr around 9–10 March 2027, subject to moon sighting. Umrah visa rules and timings can change; always confirm with the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, your Saudi partner and your airlines.

1Packages and pricing (start 3–4 months before)

  • Packages defined: first 10 days, full month, last 10 nights, Shawwal
  • Hotel names and walking distance to the Haram listed for each package
  • What's included written clearly: flights, visa, hotel, transport, ziyarat, meals
  • Group leader or scholar travelling with each group named
  • Prices for adults, children and infants; single, double, triple and quad rooms
  • Payment plan and cancellation policy written in simple language
  • Seat and room inventory per departure tracked in one sheet

2Trust signals

  • Licence or registration details shown on the website and in ad landing pages
  • Real photos and short videos from previous groups (with permission)
  • 3–5 video testimonials from past pilgrims, ideally including families and elderly travellers
  • Google Business Profile updated with photos, hours and recent reviews
  • Office address and a named contact person
  • Clear answers to family questions: wheelchair support, women's groups, medical needs

3Ads and content

  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API installed; enquiry events tested
  • Google Ads search campaign for “Umrah package from [your city]” and “Ramadan Umrah [year]”
  • At least 3 different ad creatives: package and price, real group video, family testimonial
  • Vertical videos with captions (most people watch without sound)
  • Separate ads per departure city if you fly from more than one
  • Retargeting set up for website visitors and video viewers
  • Ads for sold-out packages switched off the same day
  • Respectful tone throughout; no pushy or fear-based messaging

Month-by-month focus

WhenFocus
3–4 months before RamadanAnnounce packages, early-bird prices, build your enquiry list
2–3 months beforePackage comparisons, hotel details, past-group testimonials
1–2 months beforeSeats remaining, visa deadlines, payment plans, retargeting
Final weeksLast 10 nights and remaining seats only
During RamadanFewer sales ads; group updates, reminders and testimonial collection
After EidShawwal offers and next-season interest list

4Enquiry handling and follow-up

  • WhatsApp Business set up with a greeting message, quick replies and labels
  • Automatic reply to every web or ad enquiry within one minute
  • Call-back within 15 minutes during office hours
  • Package brochure (PDF) ready to send on WhatsApp
  • Lead sheet or CRM with status: new, contacted, quoted, deposit paid, fully paid
  • At least 5 follow-up attempts before closing an enquiry
  • Extra staff or extended hours planned for peak weeks

5Before, during and after travel

  • Pre-departure briefing (in person or online) and a document checklist for travellers
  • WhatsApp group per departure for updates
  • Emergency contact in Makkah and Madinah shared with every group
  • Permission collected before filming or photographing pilgrims
  • Review request sent within a week of return (Google review link)
  • Past pilgrims added to your list for next season and referral offers
