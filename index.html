Our story

From spreadsheets to a system.

We started with a frustration shared by every founder we knew: most agencies sell campaigns, then disappear behind confusing reports and vanity metrics.

So we built the opposite — an agency run on one Japanese idea, kaizen: continuous improvement. We don’t chase one big win. We stack hundreds of small, measured ones until the results speak for themselves.
