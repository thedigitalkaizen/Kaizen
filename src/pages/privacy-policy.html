Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information The Kaizen Agency (“we”, “us”) collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you fill in a form on this site (for example to book a strategy call, request an audit or subscribe to our newsletter), we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, company and message. When you contact us on WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive the information you share there.

Information collected automatically

We use analytics and advertising tools to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our own marketing. Depending on which tools are active, these may include:

  • Google Analytics 4 — pages visited, time on site, device and approximate location.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) — to measure our ads and show our ads to people who visited this site.
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymised heatmaps and session recordings to improve the site’s usability.

These tools use cookies or similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalised ads in your Google and Meta account settings.

When analytics or advertising tools are active, a cookie banner asks for your choice first. You can change it any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

So we know which ad or link brought you to us, your browser also stores the campaign details of your visit (for example the UTM tags and ad click ID in the link you clicked, the page you arrived on and the referring website). These details are sent along with any form you submit and are not shared with anyone else.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the services you ask about.
  • To send our newsletter, if you subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To improve our website and measure our marketing.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run this site and our business, including Formspree (form delivery), our email provider, Google, Meta and Microsoft (analytics and advertising), and WhatsApp (messaging). They process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to manage any business relationship, and delete them when no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you marketing emails. Applicable laws, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, may give you further rights.
